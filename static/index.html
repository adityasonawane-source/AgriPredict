About

AgriPredict estimates yield per hectare for a crop, state and season’s weather, then profit as yield × price minus a typical cash cost per hectare.

Yield comes from a gradient-boosting model trained on ICRISAT district data (1990–2015) for rice, pearl millet, groundnut and chickpea, using crop, state, year, season rainfall and season temperature. Soil type and humidity are not in that data, so they are rule-of-thumb adjustments. A crop sown outside its usual season takes a 40% cut.

The model reads district averages from 1990–2015, so it does not capture newer varieties or farm-level irrigation, and its rainfall response is not always smooth. Prices come from Agmarknet when a data.gov.in key is set, otherwise from typical defaults. Weather fill uses last season’s Open-Meteo data. Treat results as planning estimates.
